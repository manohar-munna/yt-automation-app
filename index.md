# yt-automation-app

A private tool that a single creator uses to upload videos to their own YouTube channels through the YouTube Data API.

- It only acts on channels whose owner signed in and approved access on Google's consent screen.
- Every video is uploaded as **private**. The channel owner reviews it and decides whether to publish it.
- It is not offered to the public, and nobody else's account can use it.
